MIDI not ready
Plug the PC straight into the Master18's USB port and choose the Master18 here (Windows lists it as DROID X7 MIDI). The Master18 USB switch must be at the right position for USB MIDI (left = USB stick mode, middle = off/reloads patch). Web MIDI needs Chrome or Edge on https or http://localhost (e.g. python -m http.server then open http://localhost:8000). Firefox asks for a site permission first.
